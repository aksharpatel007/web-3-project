Experience Ethereum scaling at its finest. Lower fees, faster transactions, and uncompromising security inherited straight from the mainnet.

The L1 Problem

Ethereum mainnet (L1) processes ~15 TPS. High demand causes network congestion, resulting in massive gas fees and slow transaction times for regular users.

Enter Arbitrum

Arbitrum is an Optimistic Rollup. It executes transactions off-chain in batches, then posts the compressed data to L1, slashing fees while maintaining Ethereum's security.

Real-World Benefit

A simple swap on Uniswap L1 might cost $20-$50 in gas. The exact same smart contract swap on Arbitrum costs mere cents and settles instantly.

Web2 vs Web3

The Evolution of the Net

Web2: Read-Write. Centralized platforms (Google, Meta) own your data and monetize it. Servers are central.


Web3: Read-Write-Own. Decentralized networks built on blockchains. You own your data via cryptography and interact trustlessly.

ETH vs BTC

Digital Gold vs World Computer

Bitcoin: A decentralized ledger designed primarily as a store of value and digital money. Simple scripting.


Ethereum: A Turing-complete platform. It processes programmable smart contracts, enabling DeFi, NFTs, and complex dApps.

Public vs Private Key

Cryptography 101

Public Key: Your bank account number. Safe to share. Used by others to send you funds or verify your signature.


Private Key: Your ATM PIN / Password. NEVER share this. It grants full mathematical control over the assets on the public key.

Blockchain vs DB

Ledgers vs Tables

Traditional DB: CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete). Controlled by admins. Fast, but requires trust in the operator.


Blockchain: Append-only. Immutable. Verified by a decentralized network of nodes. Slower, but requires zero trust.
